Know the facts

Stay informed. Stay aware. Stay safe.

Addiction rarely begins with a decision to become dependent. It usually starts with curiosity, stress, social pressure, or simply not knowing the risks. Many substances change the way the brain's reward system works, which makes stopping far harder than starting.

This site exists to share clear, judgement-free information about some of the most common addictive substances — what they are, how they affect the body and mind, and where to turn for support. Knowing what you are dealing with is one of the most effective forms of prevention there is.

Whether you are curious for yourself or worried about someone you care about, understanding comes first. Scroll down to explore each substance.
